PUT YOUR PHOTOS HERE
====================

Add JPG photos for the "custom" candle here, named exactly like this:

  custom-1.jpg
  custom-2.jpg

The website will pick these up automatically once the files exist here —
you do not need to edit any code. See README.md > 'How to add images'.

Want more than 2 photos for this candle? Open script.js, find the product
with slug: "custom", raise its imageCount, and add the matching numbered file.
