LOGO
====

Right now the site shows a simple text logo ("MUDRA CANDLES" with a small
flame icon) — you don't need to do anything for it to work.

Whenever you have a real logo, save it as:

  images/logo.png

...and it will automatically replace the text logo in the header, footer
and page-preview image. Nothing else needs to change.

Tip: a roughly square logo, at least 200x200px, on a transparent or white
background works best.
