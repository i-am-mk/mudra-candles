Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take customized orders?

Yes. Customers can discuss colors, sizes, designs, combinations and other requirements with us.

Do you offer scented candles?

Yes. Scented and unscented options are available depending on the candle/design.

Do you accept wholesale orders?

Yes. We accept wholesale and bulk requirements.

Do you take corporate orders?

Yes. We accept corporate gifting and bulk requirements.

Can I choose candle colors?

Yes. Color customization is available for suitable designs.

Do you have festival candles?

Yes. We create candles for occasions such as Diwali, Ganpati and Janmashtami, along with other festive designs.

Do you have fixed prices?

Pricing depends on quantity, combination, customization, size, scent and packaging. Contact us for a quotation.

What is the minimum order for wholesale?

Minimum quantities can vary by design and season — message us on WhatsApp with your requirement and we'll share the options available.
